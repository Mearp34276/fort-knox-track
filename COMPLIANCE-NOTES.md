# Compliance notes (high level)

This repository is a **demo track plugin and toll meter** owned by **M.E.**. It is not a licensed bank, money transmitter, lender, or payment institution.

## Honest limits

- **Operators run their own trains.** Integrating any production system (including banks or fintechs, if that is the operator’s choice) requires commercial contracts, security review, and production APIs—not this scaffold alone.
- **Money transmission and lending may be regulated.** Depending on jurisdiction and product design, operators (and sometimes intermediaries) may need licenses, partnerships, or other authorizations. Fort Knox MVP does not provide those.
- **No advice.** These notes are informational for builders reviewing the demo. They are not legal counsel.
- **No invented statutes.** We do not cite fake laws or claim specific licenses.

## What this MVP is

- A general-purpose track plugin: route id, transaction record, completion event.
- A toll meter: $0.002 USD equivalent per completed transaction, with mock/stub crypto settlement.
- A fee-comparison stub clearly labeled estimated / demo.

## What this MVP is not

- A product built only for banks or named third-party apps.
- A consumer-facing bank or app competitor.
- A guarantee of fee savings.
- Authorization to move customer funds.

Before any production use, engage counsel and compliance for the relevant markets and operator types.
